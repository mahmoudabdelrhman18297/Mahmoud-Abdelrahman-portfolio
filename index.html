About Me

Where hydraulic engineering meets BIM coordination.

My Story

I’m a BIM Coordinator and Infrastructure Civil Engineer with 6 years of experience developing and coordinating 3D models for wet and dry utilities and large infrastructure projects across Saudi Arabia and Egypt.

I combine BIM tools (Civil 3D, Navisworks, Part Builder, Autodesk Inventor) with hydraulic and hydrological analysis (SewerGEMS, WaterGEMS, HEC-HMS, GIS) so that networks are modeled accurately, sized correctly and coordinated before they reach site.

My work covers stormwater drainage, TSE and irrigation networks, groundwater drainage, potable water and sewage, including design, quantities (BOQ), shop drawings and approvals with consultants, contractors and authorities.
